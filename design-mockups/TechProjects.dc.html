Smart Shopping Cart POS

An executable point-of-sale application built in VB with the WPF framework, running on Raspberry Pi OS and synchronised to the cloud. Shoppers scan any RFID-tagged product at the cart-mounted reader and see product code, description, unit price, quantity and running total in real time. A built-in aisle locator helps find items, checkout runs through an auto-generated QR code, and the receipt is emailed automatically.
