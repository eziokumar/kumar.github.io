04 NDT & INSPECTION METHODS

Ten ways to see
inside a weld.

ASNT LEVEL IICertified to perform
SUPPORTEDDocumented in current role
VTLEVEL II
Visual Testing
Direct and remote examination of surfaces and welds for discontinuities visible to the eye.
PTLEVEL II
Liquid Penetrant
Capillary action draws penetrant into surface-breaking defects, revealed by developer.
MTLEVEL II
Magnetic Particle
Flux leakage at surface and near-surface flaws gathers magnetic particles into an indication.
UTLEVEL II
Ultrasonic Testing
High-frequency sound reflects from internal discontinuities and the back wall.
RTLEVEL II
Radiographic Testing
Radiation records internal weld structure onto film or a digital detector.
PAUTSUPPORTED
Phased Array UT
A multi-element probe steers the beam electronically to sweep the weld volume.
PWHTSUPPORTED
Post Weld Heat Treatment
Controlled heating and cooling relieves residual stress introduced by welding.
HTSUPPORTED
Hardness Testing
Indentation resistance verifies material condition and heat-treatment result.
PMISUPPORTED
Positive Material ID
Alloy composition confirmed on site against the specified material grade.
FTSUPPORTED
Ferrite Testing
Ferrite content measured in austenitic and duplex stainless steel welds.
