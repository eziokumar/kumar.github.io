The idea that

A university AR application for exploring laboratory projects, whose long-term vision was exactly the thing he ended up doing for a living: guiding technical work without an instructor standing there.

AR Application for the Faculty of Science

Point a phone or tablet at a student project in the laboratory and the application recognises it, then overlays the information that a printed label never fits. Touch a part of the AR object and that component highlights and explains itself.
