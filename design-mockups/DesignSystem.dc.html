RULES THIS DESIGN HOLDS TO
No percentage bars
Capability is grouped by domain and evidenced by roles and certificates. Fourteen bars reading 100 percent said nothing.
Certified is not supported
Six ASNT methods carry a solid amber badge. The five supported in the current role carry a plain outline. Never mixed.
Gaps stay visible
Missing facts render as a dashed amber marker, never as invented text. Nothing ships until every marker is filled or cut.
Every object earns its place
Pipe, scan ring, weld seam, film strip, gauge. No geometry exists that does not come from the trade.
